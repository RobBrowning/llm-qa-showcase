# Hire and pricing

## Bike types and prices

Wheelwise hires three types of bike. Half a day means up to four hours. The hire cost of each bike is:

- City bike, half day: £12.50
- City bike, full day: £20.00
- E-bike, half day: £25.00
- E-bike, full day: £40.00
- Cargo bike, half day: £30.00
- Cargo bike, full day: £45.00

## Multi-day hire

Hire for three days or more gets 15% off the total price. Multi-day hire must be booked online in advance.

## What is included

Every hire includes a helmet, a lock and lights at no extra cost. A child seat can be added to a city bike for £5.00 per hire.
