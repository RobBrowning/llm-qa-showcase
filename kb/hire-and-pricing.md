# Hire and pricing

## Bike types and prices

Wheelwise hires three types of bike. A city bike costs £12.50 for half a day or £20.00 for a full day. An e-bike costs £25.00 for half a day or £40.00 for a full day. A cargo bike costs £30.00 for half a day or £45.00 for a full day. Half a day means up to four hours.

## Multi-day hire

Hire for three days or more gets 15% off the total price. Multi-day hire must be booked online in advance.

## What is included

Every hire includes a helmet, a lock and lights at no extra cost. A child seat can be added to a city bike for £5.00 per hire.
