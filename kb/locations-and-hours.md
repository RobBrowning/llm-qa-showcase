# Locations and hours

## Opening hours

Wheelwise is open from 8am to 7pm, Monday to Saturday, and from 9am to 5pm on Sunday. It is closed on 25 and 26 December.

## Hubs

There are two hubs. The Harbourside hub is next to the ferry terminal. The Station hub is opposite the main entrance of the railway station. Bikes can be returned to either hub.

## Contact

For general questions, email hello@wheelwise.example or call 01632 960 400 during opening hours.
