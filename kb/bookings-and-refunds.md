# Bookings and refunds

## Booking

Bikes can be booked online up to 30 days ahead. Walk-in hire is available when bikes are free, but e-bikes often sell out at weekends.

## Deposit

A £50.00 refundable deposit is taken on a card when the bike is collected. The deposit is released within 3 working days of the bike being returned undamaged.

## Cancellations and refunds

Cancel more than 24 hours before the start of the hire for a full refund. Cancel less than 24 hours before the start and you get a 50% refund. No refund is given if you do not turn up.

## Late returns

Bikes returned late are charged £10.00 for each hour or part hour.
