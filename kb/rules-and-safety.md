# Rules and safety

## Age and ID

- Riders aged 16 or older can hire a bike on their own.
- Riders aged 12 to 15 can ride only with an adult on the same booking.
- E-bikes: riders must be 18 or older. Nobody under 18 can ride an e-bike.
- Photo ID is needed to collect any bike.

## Helmets

Helmets are provided free and are strongly recommended for every rider.

## Damage and breakdowns

If a bike breaks down, call the breakdown line on 01632 960 418 and a mechanic will come out within the city zone. Accidental damage is covered up to £200.00. Damage caused by misuse is charged at the full repair cost.
